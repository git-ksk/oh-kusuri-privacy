# Privacy Policy

**Last Updated: June 5, 2026**

This Privacy Policy explains how "Oh!MyMeds" ("the App"), developed by us ("we", "us", or "our"), handles your information.

---

## 1. Personal Information Collection and Storage

The App does not require user registration or account creation. All data entered in the App is handled as follows:

- **Local Storage Only**: Medication names, intake schedules, and intake logs are saved exclusively in the secure local storage (SwiftData) on your device.
- **No External Transmission**: We do not collect, store, or transmit your medication details or schedules to any external servers.

---

## 2. Advertisements and Data Collection

We use "Google AdMob" for in-app advertising. In connection with this, the following information may be collected automatically:

- Device ad identifiers (IDFA / IDFV)
- IP address and technical device data
- In-app ad impressions and interaction logs

This data is used solely to deliver personalized advertisements and measure ad performance. It does not contain any personally identifiable information. On iOS 14.5+, you can opt-in or opt-out of this tracking via the App Tracking Transparency (ATT) prompt.

- [Google AdMob Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy)
